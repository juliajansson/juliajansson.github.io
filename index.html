Julia Jansson Valter

PhD student in Spatial Statistics · Chalmers University of Technology

Working at the intersection of machine learning and spatial statistics, developing point process learning and spatio-temporal models for earthquake forecasting. Supervised by Ottmar Cronie and Aila Särkkä.

Google Scholar arXiv ResearchGate ORCID GitHub LinkedIn

Research

Spatial statistics studies how events are distributed across space. My work develops point process learning — a framework bridging classical spatial statistics with modern machine learning — applied to earthquake sequences, disease spread, and ecological point patterns.

Point processes Spatial statistics Machine learning Spatio-temporal models Earthquake forecasting
